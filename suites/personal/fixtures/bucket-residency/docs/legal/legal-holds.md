# Legal holds on customer data

Owner: Legal (legal-ops@quillmart.com). Questions: #legal-requests.

When a matter requires us to preserve specific customer files, Legal files an `LH-` ticket and
the media team puts a **temporary hold** on each object listed in it. A held object cannot be
deleted or overwritten until the hold is released.

Rules:

1. Only Legal can ask for a hold to be released. Engineers never release holds, not even
   temporarily to unblock a migration.
2. A hold belongs to the **data**, not the bucket: if a held object is copied or moved to
   another bucket, the copy must be put on hold too before the migration is signed off.
3. Holds are listed per matter in this folder (one object URL per line).

Active matters: see the `LH-*.txt` files next to this document.
